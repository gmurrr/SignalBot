## § 3. General refund rules

1. The User may submit a Refund request in accordance with this Refund Policy, the Terms of Service, the documents of the relevant Payment Operator and applicable law.

2. Except where a Refund is required by law, merely ceasing to use the Service, not logging in, failing to use part of a paid period, changing one's mind or deleting the Account does not automatically create a right to a Refund.

3. The absence of a particular number of Analytical Alerts, failure to achieve an expected outcome, a financial market loss, failure to enter into a transaction or the result of the User's investment decision do not by themselves constitute grounds for a Refund. The Service does not guarantee the number of Analytical Alerts, effectiveness or profits.

4. Refund requests are reviewed taking into account, in particular, the legal basis of the request, the type of plan purchased, the extent of Service use, the access activation date, reported technical issues, settlement history, transaction security and Payment Operator procedures.

5. Consideration of a voluntary Refund request does not constitute an admission of liability by the Service Provider or an obligation to grant Refunds in other similar cases.

## § 4. Statutory right of withdrawal

1. A Consumer and, where provided by law, an Entrepreneur with consumer rights may have a statutory right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days, subject to exceptions and special rules concerning Digital Services and Digital Content.

2. Where the User requests commencement of a Digital Service before the withdrawal period expires, the User may be required to pay for the service performed until the time of withdrawal, to the extent provided by law.

3. In the case of a Digital Service performed continuously, the right of withdrawal may expire only after the service has been fully performed, provided that all statutory conditions have been met, including the User's prior express consent and acknowledgement of the consequences of commencement of performance.

4. In the case of paid Digital Content not supplied on a tangible medium, the right of withdrawal may be lost once supply begins where the User has given prior express consent to commencement, acknowledged the loss of the right of withdrawal and received the required confirmation, insofar as applicable law requires those conditions.

5. Where the statutory right of withdrawal remains available, the User may submit an unequivocal withdrawal statement to **kontakt@zetasignals.pl**. The deadline is met if the statement is sent before the withdrawal period expires.

6. The statement should allow the agreement to be identified, in particular by providing the e-mail address assigned to the Account, purchase date and order or transaction identifier, if one was issued.

7. This section does not limit any other rights available under the law applicable to the User.

## § 5. Voluntary Refund requests

1. Independently of statutory rights, the User may submit a voluntary Refund request within 14 days of the payment date.

2. A voluntary Refund request is reviewed individually and does not guarantee that a Refund will be granted, unless the Refund is required by mandatory law.

3. When reviewing a voluntary request, the Service Provider may take into account, in particular:

   a) whether the request concerns the first purchase or a subsequent renewal,

   b) whether Paid Access was activated and to what extent it was used,

   c) whether the User had a genuine opportunity to use paid functions,

   d) whether a documented technical issue occurred on the Service side,

   e) whether there was a duplicate or clearly erroneous charge,

   f) whether the request may indicate abuse, fraud, circumvention of safeguards or repeated use of a paid service followed by a Refund request.

4. A voluntary Refund may be granted in full or in part depending on the circumstances, technical possibilities and the Payment Operator's rules.

## § 6. Cases in which a Refund may be granted

1. Subject to individual assessment and applicable law, a Refund may be granted in particular where:

   a) the same order was charged twice,

   b) a payment was collected despite effective cancellation of automatic renewal before the next billing period began,

   c) Paid Access was not activated due to reasons attributable to the Service or its infrastructure and was not restored within a reasonable period,

   d) paid functions were materially unavailable for a prolonged period due to reasons attributable to the Service Provider,

   e) the Digital Service or Digital Content does not conform to the agreement and the User is entitled under applicable law to a price reduction, withdrawal or termination,

   f) a Refund is required by a binding decision, applicable law or the relevant Payment Operator procedure.

2. Before granting a Refund, the Service Provider may attempt to remedy the issue, restore access or bring the Digital Service or Digital Content into conformity with the agreement where this is permitted and justified under applicable law.

## § 7. Cases in which a voluntary Refund may be refused

1. Subject to the User's mandatory rights, a voluntary Refund may be refused in particular where:

   a) the User obtained and used full access to paid functions and the request is based solely on a change of mind,

   b) the User did not use the Service for reasons attributable to the User,

   c) the User expected a particular number of Analytical Alerts, a particular effectiveness rate, profit or avoidance of loss,

   d) the request results from the outcome of a transaction or an investment decision made by the User,

   e) the User failed to cancel the Subscription before the next renewal despite clear information about automatic renewal,

   f) the issue resulted from the User's device, browser, Internet connection, broker, exchange or another service independent of the Service,

   g) the Account was suspended or deleted due to a material breach of the Terms of Service, abuse, Account sharing, unauthorised redistribution of content or actions threatening security,

   h) there are reasonable grounds indicating fraud, abuse of the Refund procedure, an unauthorised payment initiated by the User, an attempt to obtain access without payment or manipulation of the chargeback procedure.

2. Refusal of a voluntary Refund does not limit the User's rights relating to a lack of conformity of the Digital Service or Digital Content with the agreement or any other rights that cannot be excluded by contract.

## § 8. Subscription cancellation

1. Where the Service offers a Subscription, cancellation disables future automatic renewals in accordance with the rules presented to the User before purchase.

2. Cancelling a Subscription does not automatically result in a Refund for a billing period that has already begun or been paid. The User retains access until the end of the paid period unless the Terms of Service, applicable law or the Payment Operator's rules provide otherwise.

3. Merely ceasing to use the Service, not logging in, deleting an application or requesting Account deletion does not constitute cancellation of a Subscription where the Subscription is managed separately by the Payment Operator.

4. A request concerning a renewal charge may be considered voluntarily, in particular where it is submitted promptly after the charge and the User has not materially used the new access period. This does not guarantee a Refund unless required by law.

## § 9. Request procedure

1. A Refund request or withdrawal statement must be sent to **kontakt@zetasignals.pl**.

2. To allow efficient review, the User should provide:

   a) the e-mail address assigned to the Account,

   b) full name, if provided during purchase,

   c) payment date and amount,

   d) payment currency,

   e) order, payment or Subscription identifier, if issued,

   f) the reason for the request,

   g) a description of the issue and supporting materials, if available.

3. The Service Provider may request additional information necessary to confirm identity, the right to control the Account or transaction details.

4. The User should not send a full payment card number, CVV/CVC code, password, authentication codes, private key, seed phrase or any other confidential access credentials.

5. The request will be reviewed without undue delay, generally within 14 days after receipt of a complete submission, unless applicable law, the nature of the matter or the Payment Operator procedure requires a different period.

6. The User will be informed of the outcome using the e-mail address associated with the Account or specified in the request.

1. An approved Refund is generally processed using the same payment method used for the purchase where technically possible and compliant with applicable law and the Payment Operator's rules.

2. Where a Refund using the same method is not possible, the Service Provider or Payment Operator may agree with the User on another secure and lawful Refund method.

3. An approved Refund will be initiated without undue delay, generally no later than 14 days after approval, unless mandatory law requires a shorter period or the nature of the payment method or Payment Operator justifies a different period.

4. The time required for funds to be credited after a Refund is initiated may depend on the bank, card issuer, wallet provider, payment network, Payment Operator or another provider involved in settlement.

5. Where a payment was made in a currency other than the currency of the User's account, using a digital asset or a method subject to exchange-rate fluctuations, the Refund amount and currency will be determined in accordance with information provided before purchase, the Payment Operator's rules, applicable law and technical settlement possibilities.

6. Bank, network, conversion or other fees charged by third parties and not received by the Service Provider may be non-refundable unless applicable law, Payment Operator rules or an individual decision provides otherwise.

7. Following a full Refund, the Service Provider may terminate the Paid Access linked to the refunded payment. In the case of a partial Refund, the scope of continued access may be adjusted accordingly.

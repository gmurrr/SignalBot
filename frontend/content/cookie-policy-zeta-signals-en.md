## § 2. What cookies, LocalStorage and similar technologies are

1. **Cookies** are small text files stored on the User's terminal device, such as a computer, phone, tablet or other device enabling the use of the Internet.

2. Cookies may contain technical information enabling the browser to be recognized, the session to be maintained, User preferences to be remembered, security to be ensured, login to be handled or specific functions of the Service to operate properly.

3. **LocalStorage** is a browser storage mechanism that enables data to be stored locally on the User's device. Data stored in LocalStorage may remain stored even after the browser is closed, until it is deleted by the User, the Service or the browser mechanism.

4. **SessionStorage** is a browser storage mechanism similar to LocalStorage; however, data stored in SessionStorage is generally maintained only for the duration of a given browser session.

5. **Similar technologies** means other technical solutions that enable information to be stored on the User's terminal device or access to information already stored on that device, regardless of their name or technical implementation.

6. In this Cookie Policy, the term “cookies” may also include LocalStorage, SessionStorage and other similar technologies, if the context indicates that the storage of information or access to information on the User's terminal device is meant.

## § 9. How to manage cookies and LocalStorage through the browser

1. The User may independently manage cookies, LocalStorage, SessionStorage and other site data through the settings of the web browser.

2. In particular, the User may delete existing cookies and site data, block cookies, block third-party cookies, delete LocalStorage and SessionStorage data, set automatic deletion of data after closing the browser or use private/incognito mode.

3. 4. Below are example paths for managing cookies in popular browsers. Setting names may vary depending on browser version, operating system or interface language.

### 1) Google Chrome

1. Open Chrome. Click the three-dot icon in the upper-right corner. Go to **Settings**. Go to **Privacy and security**. Select **Third-party cookies** or **Site settings**. Manage consents, block cookies or delete site data. To delete data for a specific site, go to site data settings and search for the Service domain.

### 2) Mozilla Firefox

1. Open Firefox. Click the menu icon in the upper-right corner. Go to **Settings**. Go to **Privacy & Security**. In the **Cookies and Site Data** section, select the relevant settings. You can delete data, manage exceptions or block selected tracking mechanisms.

### 3) Safari

1. Open Safari. Go to **Settings** or **Preferences**. Open the **Privacy** tab. Manage cookies, site data and tracking settings. You can delete data for selected websites or block specific mechanisms.

### 4) Microsoft Edge

1. Open Microsoft Edge. Click the three-dot icon in the upper-right corner. Go to **Settings**. Go to **Cookies and site permissions**. Select **Manage and delete cookies and site data**. Delete data, block third-party cookies or set exceptions for selected websites.

5. The User may also delete data stored in LocalStorage and SessionStorage using browser developer tools, although this method is intended primarily for technical users.

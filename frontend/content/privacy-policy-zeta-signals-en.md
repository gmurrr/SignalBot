## § 5. Purposes and legal bases of data processing

1. Users’ personal data are processed for the following purposes and on the following legal bases:

### 1) Creating and maintaining an Account

Scope of data: e-mail address, password in secured form, User identifier, technical data related to login.

Purpose: creating an Account, enabling login, managing access to the Service and performing the electronic service.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, i.e. processing necessary for the performance of a contract or to take steps prior to entering into a contract.

### 2) Handling the Subscription and access to paid functions

Scope of data: e-mail address, User identifier, Subscription status, transaction identifier, customer or subscription identifier in Lemon Squeezy.

Purpose: activating the Subscription, verifying payment, managing access to paid functions of the Service and handling the automatically renewable Subscription.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### 3) Handling payments through Lemon Squeezy

Scope of data: e-mail address, transaction data, order identifier, Subscription identifier, payment status.

Purpose: linking the payment to the User’s Account, activating or deactivating access to paid functions of the Service, handling refunds, chargebacks and settlements.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, i.e. the Controller’s legitimate interest consisting in payment handling, fraud prevention and protection against claims.

### 4) Ensuring the security of the Service

Scope of data: IP address, browser data, device technical data, system logs, login attempt data, timestamps, technical error data.

Purpose: ensuring the security of the Service, detecting abuse, protecting against unauthorised access, protecting against attacks, diagnosing errors and ensuring stable operation of the Service.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, i.e. the Controller’s legitimate interest consisting in ensuring the security, integrity and stability of the Service.

### 5) Handling requests, complaints and correspondence

Scope of data: e-mail address, message content, data voluntarily provided by the User, correspondence history.

Purpose: responding to an inquiry, handling a complaint, conducting correspondence, explaining technical or payment-related issues.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR if the correspondence concerns performance of the contract, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR if the basis is the Controller’s legitimate interest consisting in handling contact and defending against claims.

### 6) Compliance with legal obligations

Scope of data: transaction data, settlement data, data necessary to document performance of the service, data required by law or authorised authorities.

Purpose: fulfilment of obligations arising from legal provisions, in particular tax, accounting, consumer or personal data protection obligations.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

### 7) Pursuing claims and defending against claims

Scope of data: Account data, transaction data, system logs, history of document acceptance, Subscription history, correspondence, technical data.

Purpose: establishing, pursuing or defending against claims, including claims concerning payments, use of the Service, complaints, chargebacks, breaches of the Terms of Service or legal disputes.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, i.e. the Controller’s legitimate interest.

### 8) Documenting acceptance of terms, consents and declarations

Scope of data: e-mail address, User identifier, date and time of acceptance, document version, IP address, user agent, transaction or Subscription identifier.

Purpose: proving that the User read the applicable documents, accepted the required terms, received information on risk, subscription, withdrawal rights and rules of operation of the Service.

Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
